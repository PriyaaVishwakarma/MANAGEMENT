About Us

A reliable partner for security, facility & manpower services

We are known as a reliable service provider for Housekeeping Services, Security Services, IT professionals for BPO, and manpower for export-import business houses — and we undertake construction work too. Our services are implemented by trained staff who make use of a state-of-the-art process, equipment and eco-friendly products.

Our consultants are aware that every client's situation is unique. We work closely to meet your specific requirements, and our role is to work with our clients and candidates in complete confidentiality, enabling us to provide the best possible options.

Our vision is to place our company among the top ten security service providers in India, measured by the quality of security delivered rather than by profit alone. We serve PSUs, MNCs, business & industrial groups, office complexes, posh residential areas, hotels, clubs, corporate buildings, farms and under-development areas.

Recruitment & Operations

How we screen, train and supervise our guards

Senior officers from the armed forces run initial screening; a selection team headed by the director makes the final call. Every guard clears a vocabulary test, personality profile, background check and in-depth interview before pre-assignment training.

1

Screening

Vocabulary test, personality questionnaire, background check & interview.

2

Training

Pre-assignment training by certified instructors; test on every subject.

3

Deployment

Min. 10-12 pass, 5'7"+ height, medically fit, national language.

4

Supervision

Daily briefing by Post Supervisor, daily checks by Field Supervisor.

5

Monitoring

24x7 control room, monthly fire/evacuation drills, QRT for disasters.
